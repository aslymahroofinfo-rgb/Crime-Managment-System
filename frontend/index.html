Stay Safe

Public Awareness & Safety Tips

Follow these essential safety practices to protect yourself and your community.

Never Share OTPs

No bank, government agency, or officer will ever ask for your OTP. Keep it strictly private.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you notice something unusual in your area, report it immediately to local authorities.

Protect Personal Info

Avoid sharing Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details with unknown callers or websites.

Verify Online Transactions

Always verify the seller's identity before making any online payment or transfer.
