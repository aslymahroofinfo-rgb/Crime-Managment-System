Public Safety Portal

About Our System

A transparent digital platform dedicated to securing public safety, streamlining reporting, and building stronger community partnerships.

Our Mission

To empower citizens with a safe, secure, and easily accessible online platform to report incidents, track case progression, and foster trust and transparency between the public and local law enforcement bodies.

Our Vision

To build a safer, crime-resilient nation where technology connects communities and safety services, providing instantaneous support, real-time tracking, and verified justice for every individual.

Core Drivers

System Objectives

The primary structural goals governing the development of the Crime Investigation Website.

Secure Anonymity

Allowing victims or whistleblowers to report incidents anonymously with top-tier data security encryption.

Accelerated Triage

Minimizing paper filing bottlenecks by routing citizen complaints directly to dispatch officers.

Progress Accountability

Providing continuous digital logs so citizens stay informed of each milestone in investigations.

Data Integrity

Eliminating file tampering and loss through unified, immutable digital database registries.

Why Use CIW?

Traditional crime reporting requires physically visiting station houses, standing in queues, and filling out offline forms. CIW digitizes this workflow to solve safety reporting bottlenecks:

  • Instant Filing: Bypass lines. Submit comprehensive statements and location details from any device in under five minutes.
  • Unified Evidence Uploads: Directly attach CCTV still captures, voice recordings, document files, or photos.

Benefits for Citizens

Our portal is designed with the safety, comfort, and accessibility requirements of general citizens in mind:

  • Real-Time Case Notifications: Know exactly when your case gets approved, which officer is assigned, and when it is resolved.
  • Guaranteed Anonymity: Citizens can hide their identifying profile to protect personal safety while still reporting public hazards.
